Why this is safe

Lifeline doesn't ask donors to trust an AI's opinion. It uses AI to gather evidence, deterministic rules to decide, and PayPal to make sure money can only move to registered local relief charities after the evidence passes.

Lifeline verifies

  • The disaster declaration (FEMA)
  • The satellite flood extent
  • The place and the date window
  • Registered, eligibility-screened local charities (IRS register, Pub 78, revocations, OFAC)

Lifeline does not verify

  • The person who posted
  • The family
  • The exact need
  • How the charity spends the money

What protects the donor

  • The person who posted the appeal is never paid
  • The PayPal authorization is voided if the checks fail
  • Money only goes to registered local charities
  • Every receipt is hashed

Still needs a human

  • UNVERIFIABLE cases
  • The charity's follow-up with a family
